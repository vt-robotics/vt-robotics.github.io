Robotics at Virginia Tech

Welcome to robotics at Virginia Tech! We advance robotics research from theory to practice. Our faculty and students work in labs across the university, spanning robotics topics: from biological inspiration to flight, from human-robot interaction to assistive devices, and from foundation models to agentic systems. Overall, our goal is to develop the fundamental technology that will make robots useful.
