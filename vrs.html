Virginia Robotics Symposium

The Virginia Robotics Symposium (VRS) brings together robotics researchers from across the Commonwealth. It is a one-day event with research talks, poster presentations, and social activities. The goal is to build connections between robotics groups in Virginia and to share new ideas.

VRS is organized by faculty at Virginia Tech, the University of Virginia, and George Mason University, and the host campus changes each year. Speakers, schedules, and registration are on the symposium website.
